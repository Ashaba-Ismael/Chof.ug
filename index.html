About Us

What is CHOF Uganda (Children's Hope Foundation Uganda)

Children’s Hope Foundation (CHOF) is a nonprofit organization located in southwestern Uganda. It was founded in 2024. It is registered with local government under registration number ………/2026. CHOF was founded with a purpose of solving conservation challenges in southwestern Uganda while eradicating household poverty among members, promoting community welfare, health and sensitizing children and Youth on the development programs

Origin of CHOF Uganda

The existence of CHOF Uganda emerged from the community suffering as a result of protected natural resources among the area.

Ibambiro community is within the boundaries of Queen Elizabeth national park. For the people living within this community are being seen as problems instead of looking at them as an opportunity. The reasons are multiple whereby wild animals in Queen Elizabeth destroy their gardens, attack their temporary households and sometimes kill people and their domestic animals in the community. This also affects their access to health care because of walking long distances to health facilities is dangerous and hiring vehicles is expensive too. Child labor, family neglect and early pregnancies among children and Youth.

Because of these challenges, communities have neglected conservation of these natural resources. A number of members decided to do something about it and formed SACY Uganda as a local community based organization to work with communities to conserve the nature for the benefit for us all and empower the local community with the resources to help them.
